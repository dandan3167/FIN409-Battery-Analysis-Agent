# Formal English-run evidence package

207 source/evidence records reused from the previously verified local library. No report or expected rating is included. Original quotations and units may be Chinese; preserve them in source mappings and translate only the report prose. Source assets are unchanged copies of the originals/extracts named by source locators, not newly retrieved or comprehensively re-extracted PDFs.

Facts, calculations, inferred approximations, source assessments, missing values and scope exclusions remain distinct. Management or institution assessments are source evidence, not independent causal proof. Development provenance and two pieces of earlier analyst framing were separated outside this package; all 207 facts and their counterevidence remain.

Read the source-specific limitations. Metadata may still say geography/scope must be read from the original. A source can be wider than China vehicle batteries; it does not redefine the target market. Short-name/legal-entity correspondence, publication dates and procurement denominators can remain unverified. Do not assume certainty.
