# Evidence and reasoning rules

Use only Porter's Five Forces. The target is China's broad vehicle traction-battery manufacturing and automotive supply industry in 2023–2025, with CATL as the focal company. Both external automotive supply and captive internal supply matter. Stationary storage is outside the target market, although a source may contain broader background evidence that must be qualified.

1. Classify every input before using it: direct source disclosure, deterministic calculation, inferred approximation, source assessment, missing value, or non-comparable business scope. Original Chinese quotations remain authoritative. Attribute a company's performance claims or an institution's assessment; neither is an independently established causal effect. Translate faithfully without upgrading certainty.
2. Check observation period separately from publication date. A later publication may verify 2025, but later operations and forecasts are not research-period outcomes. Preserve geography, product, transaction flow, denominators, units, original business name and measurement precision. A missing item means not found in the searched materials, not that it does not exist.
3. Rivalry concerns actual constraints on orders, costs/performance and value capture. Demand growth only offers a possible relief mechanism unless effective supply is also checked. High concentration, a leader's share or margins, and non-synchronised sample margins do not prove weak competition. Material costs, manufacturing efficiency and mix can also explain price declines. Category averages are not matched-product net prices. Broad cell capacity or a company utilisation ratio is not the target industry's utilisation.
4. Buyer power concerns purchase scale, qualified alternatives, self-production feasibility and mutual dependence from development, validation, quality and delivery. Group customer concentration is auxiliary and not China's battery-buyer concentration. Do not identify anonymous customers or link annual ranks as the same entity. A customer appearing in two supplier disclosures does not establish immediate switching on the same vehicle platform. Rebates do not establish buyer dominance. Cooperation does not prove lock-in. A self-production case does not describe all automakers; distinguish joint ventures from independent production.
5. Supplier power differs across resources, processed materials and equipment. Commodity prices, supply risk and bargaining power are different variables. Country concentration is not firm concentration or CATL's procurement dependence. Group procurement denominators remain incompletely defined; unexplained changes cannot determine a rating. Material-cost percentages and amounts must both be considered. Long agreements need not fix prices; investment is not operation; processing capacity, processing volume, recovered salts, lithium metal and self-sufficiency are distinct.
6. Entry threats differ between independent firms lacking manufacturing/customer foundations and entrants with automaker or industrial backing. Explain how financing becomes reliable production, yields, qualification, supply organisation and sustained commercial scale. Incumbent annual spending illustrates capabilities, not a universal minimum entry cost. Established suppliers' expansion is primarily rivalry, even through a new legal subsidiary. Distinguish establishment, agreement, construction, trial production, commissioning, delivery, replacement of previous purchases and sustained scale. Internal supply may affect external demand, but its replacement effect needs evidence. A verified single case can improve confidence in that fact without automatically improving confidence in an industry-wide rating. Verify short-name/entity correspondence rather than assuming exact legal identity.
7. Substitutes are external ways of providing transport functionality. LFP, NMC, sodium-ion and solid-state are internal battery technologies. Swapping and fast charging usually change use conditions rather than eliminate batteries. Combustion-mechanical power can serve the same transport task; ordinary hybrids, PHEVs, range extenders and fuel-cell vehicles may retain batteries and change demand intensity. Use actual configurations and do not generalise one truck to every fuel-cell vehicle. Vehicle counts are not battery GWh. Non-NEVs are not all pure combustion vehicles; NEVs are not all BEVs. Sales including exports are not domestic consumer purchase shares. Preserve passenger/commercial distinctions and the year, segment and price definition of comparisons; purchase price is not total ownership cost. Unverified public/shared-transport net effects are not decisive.
8. For each force distinguish observed facts, mechanisms, counterweights, alternative explanations, limitations, rating and confidence. Ratings must arise from weighing the active evidence, not from a default, a count of indicators, an arbitrary numerical score, or a previous result. Missing evidence mainly affects confidence, but loss of support for a decisive mechanism can also change a judgment. Equal force ratings are allowed. Confidence in one fact is not confidence in the whole force.
9. Synthesize industry attractiveness through profitability/value-capture mechanisms, their interaction, stage and application context; never average ordinal force ratings. Assess CATL's competitive position separately. Market leadership and relatively high margins can indicate conditions for responding to pressure, but do not prove weaker industry rivalry, lower pressure received, cost leadership or pricing power.
10. Do not double-count a shared fact as independent corroboration. Automaker self-production can explain buyer alternatives and entry, but the same event is one connected fact cluster. Document/page overlap and report identity matter. A shared archive container may hold different reports, so use the actual publication and URL as well as document metadata.
11. Keep 2023 original financial disclosures separate from subsequent comparative layers. Prefer directly disclosed 2024 margin changes. Back-calculated comparative margins from rounded percentages are approximations, not directly disclosed facts. Do not derive spurious precise adjusted costs or allocate the entire group warranty reclassification to traction batteries. Different companies' globally scoped product businesses are auxiliary comparisons. BYD's mixed automotive segment is not standalone battery financial evidence. Never divide China's installed GWh by global company revenue to construct a unit price.
12. Give each key factual claim a traceable active evidence ID and source locator. The adjacent quotation must support the actual claim. Keep reported shares and shares recomputed using industry totals separate; CR2/3/5 are sums of disclosed ranking shares, with cross-year scope caveats. Retain counterevidence even if inconvenient. Mechanical checks and same-model semantic review are complementary and neither proves that a rating is correct.

No force rating, confidence label, industry-attractiveness verdict or CATL-position verdict is prescribed by this method.
